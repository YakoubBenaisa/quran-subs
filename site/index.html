Three ways to use it

Listen, memorize, or follow along

With reciter

Pick one of 115 reciters. Each verse appears as it is recited, and long verses turn their page on the exact word for reciters that have word timings.

Memorize

Silent, at your pace: by verse length, a fixed time, or a reciter's real timing. Show the first words as a hint, or show the verse and then hide it so you recite from memory.

Manual

The text moves only when you press a key. Handy in a lesson, or while a recitation plays somewhere else.

Made for long hours

Stays out of your way, all day

The verses never take focus and never block a click. Everything is set once in a calm settings window, in Arabic or English.

Any recitation you love

Paste a YouTube or MP3 link, or choose a file. The app matches the recording against reciters with known timings and finds where each verse starts. A recording that is a published one comes out almost exact. If a verse is late, press one key the moment it starts and the rest re-aligns.

115

Reciters with verse timings

From mp3quran.net, searchable in Arabic and English. Download any of them for offline use.

Private by design

Nothing about you leaves your computer

  • No account and no tracking. Settings and recordings stay in your user folder.
  • Listening stays in memory. To follow a browser video, the app briefly compares your speakers' sound with the recitation. It never uses the microphone, and records or sends nothing.
  • Open sources. Text and translations from alquran.cloud, recitations and timings from mp3quran.net, word timings from quran.com.
